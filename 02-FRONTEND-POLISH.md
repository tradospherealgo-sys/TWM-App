# Frontend / Product Polish Team

Audit every route and major component for:
- hierarchy, spacing, typography
- responsive/mobile behavior
- navigation
- tables/cards/charts
- loading, empty and error states
- accessibility
- focus/hover/disabled states
- placeholder/demo copy
- inconsistent terminology
- console/runtime errors

## Button Audit
Trace every button/link to its actual handler, route or API. If it appears actionable but does nothing, flag it. Recommend removal or a real implementation.

## Branding
Check Tradosphere logo, favicon, title, metadata, OG branding, consistent naming and absence of accidental template/competitor branding.

Never invent a logo. If the approved logo is missing, report exactly where it is needed.
